ARTICLE AUDIO · ON YOUR DEVICE

Good reads.
Out loud.

Listen to articles with eight English voices, entirely inside Chrome. No account, server, or uploads.

Margin starts listening when the first passage is ready, generates the rest locally, and lets you save the full narration as a WAV. Choose your voice and narration speed, or paste a passage you want to hear.

A little room to listen.

  1. Open Margin's side panel beside an article.
  2. Choose “Read this tab” or paste text.
  3. Pick a voice and choose “Make audio.”

Keep the side panel open while generating and listening. Speech generation works offline with the bundled model; reading an article requires access to its page.
